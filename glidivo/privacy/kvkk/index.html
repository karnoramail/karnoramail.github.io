English — Privacy notice under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK)

This notice informs the players of GLIDIVO, and the parents of children who play, as required by Article 10 of Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The short notice shown when the game is first opened summarises it; the "I have read it and I accept" button records that you were informed and is not a separate explicit consent.

1. Data controller

Karnora Games (Majorworx d.o.o.). Requests and questions: onurmajorworx@gmail.com.

2. What is processed, and how

  • Game data: progress, points (XP), level, settings, finished stages, best times, the list of purchased items, the age group derived from the birth year asked before a purchase (only "under 13 / 13 and over"; the birth year is not stored) and the date this notice was accepted. This data is stored only on your device; the game does not send it to any developer server. If your device backup is on (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone) it is included in the backup in your own account; the developer cannot access these backups.
  • Purchases: made through Google Play or the App Store. Payment details never reach the game or the developer; the game keeps only the product and transaction information the store reports, on the device.
  • Ads: the game shows Google AdMob ads (an interstitial between races and optional rewarded ads). Every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment, only general-audience (G) and non-personalised ads are requested, and the Android app removes the advertising ID permission. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google automatically collects, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app, device and other identifiers, approximate location derived from the IP address, app and ad interactions and diagnostics, and processes them under its own policy.
  • E-mail: if you e-mail us, we use your address and message only to answer your request.
  • Not collected: the game has no account; it does not ask for or collect a name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone data. The optional tilt steering uses the motion sensor on the device only; that data is neither stored nor sent.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Running the game, keeping progress and settings, delivering and restoring purchased items: KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) (necessary for the formation or performance of a contract — providing the game and the items bought).
  • Showing child-appropriate, non-personalised ads so that the game can be free, measuring them and preventing fraud: KVKK Art. 5(2)(f) (the controller's legitimate interest, provided the data subject's fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed).
  • Answering your requests and questions: KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and 5(2)(f).

4. Recipients and transfers

Ad data goes to Google LLC and its affiliates (Google AdMob); purchases are handled by Google LLC (Google Play) or Apple Inc. (App Store). Their servers may be outside Türkiye; transfer and processing take place under these companies' own policies and safeguards (e.g. standard contracts). Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 · Apple privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. The developer does not sell or pass personal data to anyone else.

5. Children

The audience includes children. Children are not asked for personal details; ads are served with child-directed settings. Players under 13 need a parent's approval before every purchase. We ask parents to read this notice with their children and to send us any questions.

6. Retention

Game data on the device is kept until the app is removed or the backup is deleted in the device or account settings. Data processed by Google and Apple follows their own retention periods. E-mails are kept only as long as needed after your request has been answered.

7. Your rights under KVKK Article 11

By applying to the data controller you may: learn whether your personal data is processed; request information about it; learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used accordingly; know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request its erasure or destruction under the conditions of Article 7; request that correction, erasure or destruction be notified to the third parties it was transferred to; object to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. Send your request in writing to onurmajorworx@gmail.com; requests are answered free of charge within thirty days at the latest (KVKK Art. 13). You can delete the game data on your device by removing the app and the backups in your device or account settings; the developer holds no player account to delete. Google's privacy tools are available for the ad data Google processes.

8. Changes

If this notice changes, the current version is published on this page and the game shows the new version again when it is next opened.
